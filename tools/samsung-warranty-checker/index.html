Samsung Warranty Checker

Enter a device's IMEI to look up its Samsung warranty status, straight from Samsung's own support lookup.

Usually 15 digits.

    Your browser calls Samsung's public warranty lookup
    (pages.samsung.com/support-api/.../products/warranty) directly — the IMEI you enter goes straight
    from your browser to Samsung, nothing passes through this site or is stored anywhere. This is an unofficial
    tool, not made or endorsed by Samsung; the endpoint is undocumented and can change or disappear without
    notice, which is why every field below is rendered generically rather than assuming a fixed shape — anything
    Samsung's response contains will show up, known fields just get friendlier labels. Results are only as
    accurate as Samsung's own records.
