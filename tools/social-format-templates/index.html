Social Ad & Post Size Templates

Pick a platform and format, see it at the exact pixel size, and download a blank canvas to design on — as PNG or PDF.

Every size below is taken from each platform's own current help/business documentation, not a third-party cheat sheet — sources are noted where a platform states a best-practice figure rather than one fixed official spec.
