How this works, and its real limits:

  • Browsers don't let a page resize the tab you're already reading this in — that's a deliberate security restriction. "Open sized popup window" instead opens a new window sized to compensate for that window's toolbars/borders, so its content area lands on the target size. If your browser blocks the popup, allow popups for this site and click again.
  • Some browsers (particularly Safari, and any window with more than one tab) restrict script-driven resizing further and may ignore the correction — if the readout above doesn't turn green after opening the popup, drag its corner while watching the live numbers; they update in real time.
  • On a phone or tablet there's no OS-level window to resize, so this only meaningfully works on desktop.
  • The "full-size guide overlay" always works, in any window, on any device — it draws a real-pixel-accurate box you can align a manual screen-recording crop against, without needing to resize anything.
  • HiDPI/Retina displays: your browser reports viewport size in CSS pixels, but most screen recorders capture at CSS pixels × your display's pixel ratio. On a typical 2× Retina screen, a 1920×1080 viewport is usually captured as 3840×2160 raw pixels unless your recording software downscales — check your recorder's output setting if the exact pixel count matters.
