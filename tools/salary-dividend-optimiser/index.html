Salary vs Dividend Extraction Optimiser

Compare every feasible split of a company profit pool between director's salary/bonus and dividends, for a single director-shareholder.

Illustrative estimator only — not tax or accounting advice. This tool models a simplified single-director, single-shareholder company against the assumptions and exclusions listed at the bottom of this page. Dividends can only lawfully be paid from adequate distributable reserves and require proper company authorisation (board minutes and dividend vouchers) — this tool does not check reserves beyond the current profit pool entered, and does not know your company's actual accounts. Always confirm your figures with a qualified accountant before acting on them.

    Excluded from this version: pension contributions, benefits in kind, student loan repayments, IR35/off-payroll working, multiple shareholders or share classes, and prior retained reserves (the pool entered is treated as the entire distributable resource under consideration — it does not add to or draw down any existing reserves in the company's accounts). Corporation Tax marginal relief assumes augmented profits equal basic profits (i.e. the company receives no dividends from other companies). Personal Allowance taper, Corporation Tax limits, and dividend-band thresholds are captured via the calculation step size rather than exact algebraic snapping (only National Insurance and Employment Allowance thresholds are snapped exactly) — reduce the step size for more precision near those boundaries.
