UK Location Facts

Enter a UK postcode, town, or use your location — see what UK government open data says about that place.

Location resolved via postcodes.io. Crime and neighbourhood policing data from data.police.uk (Home Office). MP lookup from the UK Parliament API. Flood data from the Environment Agency. Bank holidays from GOV.UK. Related datasets from the data.gov.uk CKAN catalogue. All are free, key-free UK government (or government-run open data) sources — everything here is looked up live and nothing you search is stored. Crime figures lag by a month or two, as noted against each figure.
