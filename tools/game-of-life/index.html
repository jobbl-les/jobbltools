Conway's Game of Life

Starts spelling "TOOLS", then evolves under the standard rules. Click any cell to toggle it.

Conway's Game of Life: each cell lives or dies each generation based on its eight neighbours — a live cell with fewer than two or more than three live neighbours dies, a dead cell with exactly three live neighbours comes to life, and everything else stays as it is (the classic "B3/S23" rule). The board wraps at the edges (a torus), so patterns like gliders cycle around indefinitely instead of running off the edge. Text patterns like "TOOLS" are not stable structures, so don't be surprised when it dissolves into blinkers and still lifes within a few generations — that's normal. Read more on Wikipedia.
