Everything runs in your browser — files and pasted text are never uploaded anywhere. Detection: any bare 15-digit number that passes the IMEI Luhn check digit is treated as an IMEI (hyphens, dots, and spaces within a number are ignored). Mobile numbers are matched as UK local (07…, 11 digits), UK international without a plus (447…, 12 digits), or any +-prefixed number of 8–15 digits — all normalised to +<digits> form in the results, with duplicates (even across different formats of the same number) collapsed and counted. This is a lightweight pattern match, not a full international phone-number parser: numbers using "(0)" trunk-prefix notation, or non-UK domestic formats without a leading +, won't be picked up.
