Gilt Cashflow & Tax Calculator

Pick a gilt, enter an amount, see every cashflow to redemption — and how much of any gain is tax-free capital vs taxable coupon income.

Illustrative estimator only — not investment or tax advice. Prices are from the London Stock Exchange, delayed at least 15 minutes and refreshed once daily — not a live dealing price. Excludes any bid/offer spread and broker/platform fees. Confirm the actual price with your broker before dealing. Only conventional (fixed-coupon) gilts are covered — index-linked gilts' future coupons and redemption value depend on RPI inflation between now and each payment date, which can't be projected here. Buying between coupon dates (the usual case) means paying the seller accrued interest on top of the quoted price — included in the cost/nominal figures below — but the full next coupon received is shown as your taxable income; in reality HMRC's Accrued Income Scheme taxes the pre-purchase slice of it to the seller instead, which isn't modelled here.

Capital element — tax-free
—
Gilts are exempt from Capital Gains Tax on disposal or redemption (TCGA 1992 s.115) — whether this is a gain (bought below par) or a loss (bought above par).
Coupon income — taxable
—
Taxed as savings income at your marginal rate. Your Personal Savings Allowance (£1,000 basic rate / £500 higher rate / £0 additional rate) and the starting rate for savings may cover some or all of it — not modelled here since it depends on your other income.
